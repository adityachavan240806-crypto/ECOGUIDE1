Waste Reduction Tips

Small habits, big impact

Reducing waste starts before something becomes trash.

🛍️

Choose reusables

Carry a reusable bag, bottle, and cup to avoid single-use items in your daily routine.

🔧

Repair before replacing

Fixing electronics, clothes, and furniture extends their life and keeps valuable materials in use.

🧼

Clean before recycling

Rinse containers and remove food residue so recyclable materials don't contaminate the batch.

📦

Donate what works

Items in good condition can be donated, sold, or given away instead of being thrown out.

🪫

Handle hazardous waste carefully

Batteries, paint, and e-waste need special drop-off points - never put them in regular bins.

🌿

Compost organics

Food scraps and yard waste can become nutrient-rich compost instead of landfill methane.

About EcoGuide

Make the responsible choice easy

EcoGuide helps you quickly understand what to do with everyday items. By choosing reuse or proper recycling, you reduce landfill waste, conserve natural resources, and lower pollution.

The 3Rs

  • Reduce - Buy and use only what you need.
  • Reuse - Give items another purpose before discarding.
  • Recycle - Turn materials into new products responsibly.
